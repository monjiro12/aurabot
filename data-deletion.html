User Data Deletion Instructions

Users may request deletion of data associated with their AURA account or connected integrations using any of the methods below.

Before publishing: Replace the placeholder email addresses and add your actual target processing period.
01

Method 1 — Email request

Send a deletion request to:

privacy@yourdomain.com

Subject: AURA Data Deletion Request

To help us locate the relevant information, include:

  • Your name
  • Email associated with the account
  • Connected business or Page name, if applicable
  • A brief description of the deletion request
Start email request
02

Method 2 — Disconnect integration

You may disconnect supported third-party integrations through the relevant provider or account settings. Disconnecting may stop future access by AURA, subject to the provider's controls.

Disconnecting an integration may not automatically remove information already stored in AURA. To request deletion of stored data, also use Method 1 or Method 3.

Review integration controls
03

Method 3 — Contact support

Contact product support for help submitting or clarifying a deletion request:

support@yourdomain.com

Once identity or ownership is reasonably verified, associated data will be reviewed for deletion subject to necessary legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution retention obligations.

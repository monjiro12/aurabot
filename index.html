Responsible connectivity

Meta integration with clear user control

AURA can integrate with Meta products where supported. Users authenticate with accounts they are authorized to use and may only access Pages or business assets for which they have appropriate permissions.

  • Data is used to provide functionality requested by the user.
  • AURA does not sell authentication credentials.
  • Credentials and access tokens must be handled securely.
  • Users can disconnect integrations and request deletion of associated data.

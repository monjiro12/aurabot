1. Introduction

AURA Data Intelligence ("AURA," "we," "us," or "our") provides software that helps authorized business users organize, analyze, and manage permitted customer interaction and social data. This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through the AURA website, product, support channels, and supported integrations.

By using AURA, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you use AURA on behalf of a business, that business may separately control information processed in its workspace.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use AURA, the features you enable, and the permissions granted through connected services.

Account Information

We may collect identifiers and business contact details such as your name, email address, organization name, role, account preferences, and support communications.

Integration Information

When you connect a supported third-party platform, we may receive identifiers for the connected account, Page, or business asset; granted permission information; integration status; and access credentials or tokens needed to provide requested functionality. Production credentials must be handled securely and are not published on this website.

Business and Customer Interaction Data

At an authorized business user's direction, AURA may process permitted information associated with business assets and customer interactions, such as conversation metadata, message content made available through approved functionality, public engagement information, lead records, campaign organization data, and related business notes. The exact data depends on platform permissions and the features used.

Technical Information

We may collect technical and usage information needed to operate and secure the service, such as device and browser type, IP address, timestamps, diagnostic events, feature usage, and security logs. This static public website does not include analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or cookies by default.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve requested AURA functionality.
  • Authenticate users and maintain authorized integrations.
  • Organize, analyze, display, and export permitted business data.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and business inquiries.
  • Protect users, business workspaces, and the service from misuse or security threats.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.
  • Communicate important product, security, or policy updates.

4. Legal and Business Purposes

Depending on the context and applicable law, information may be processed to perform a contract, provide functionality requested by a user, pursue legitimate business interests such as service security and improvement, comply with legal obligations, or act with consent where required. Business customers are responsible for identifying their own lawful basis and obtaining necessary permissions for data they place in AURA.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell authentication credentials. We may share information only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with law, protect rights and security, complete a corporate transaction, or follow an authorized user's direction. We do not describe AURA as having access beyond the permissions made available by connected platforms.

Service Providers

Vendors that support hosting, security, communications, or technical operations may process limited information on our behalf under appropriate contractual and confidentiality obligations. A current production deployment should identify the providers actually used where required.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, maintaining security, resolving disputes, meeting legal obligations, and enforcing agreements. Retention periods may vary by information type, workspace configuration, connected platform requirements, and applicable law.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their accounts, use secure authentication practices, limit permissions appropriately, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

8. User Rights

Depending on your location and relationship with AURA, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a copy of certain personal information. You may also be able to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. We may need to verify your identity, authority, or relationship to a business workspace before acting on a request.

9. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with your AURA account or connected integration by following our User Data Deletion Instructions. We may retain limited information where reasonably necessary for legal, fraud prevention, security, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations.

10. Third-Party Platforms

AURA may connect with third-party platforms, including supported Meta products, at the user's direction. Those platforms operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their practices are not controlled by this policy. Users must only connect accounts and business assets they are authorized to access and must comply with applicable platform requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

AURA is designed for business users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children through this website. If you believe a child has provided information directly to us, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

12. International Users

If you use AURA from outside the country where its operators or service providers are located, information may be processed in other jurisdictions. Privacy and data protection rules may differ across countries. Production operations should implement transfer safeguards where required by applicable law.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service, legal requirements, or operational practices change. The revised effective date will be posted at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through other appropriate channels.

Supported platform connectivity

Meta Platform Integration

AURA may use supported Meta technologies to help authorized business users connect eligible business assets and organize relevant customer engagement information.

01

Purpose

Supported integrations allow authorized business users to connect business assets and use permitted information within requested AURA features.

02

Authorization

Users must authenticate with their own authorized accounts and must not connect accounts, Pages, or assets they are not permitted to use.

03

Permissions

AURA only intends to request permissions needed for the features enabled by the user. Availability depends on platform approval and configuration.

04

Data Access

Data accessed through Meta services should be used only to provide requested functionality and handled according to applicable platform requirements.

05

User Control

Users can stop using the service, disconnect supported integrations, request associated data deletion, or contact support for help.

06

Security

Tokens and private credentials must be stored securely in production systems and must never be exposed in front-end code or public repositories.

Permission boundaries

The connected business stays in control

AURA is designed around access authorized by the user and made available by the connected platform. It is not positioned as a way to bypass platform restrictions or obtain private data without permission.

  • Authenticate only with an account you are authorized to use.
  • Select only supported Pages and business assets available to you.
  • Grant only the permissions needed for enabled functionality.
  • Disconnect access when it is no longer required.
